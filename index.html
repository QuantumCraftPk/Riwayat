A Feast Steeped in Tradition

Riwayat means tradition. Our kitchens honour the grand dastarkhwan of the Mughal table — slow-cooked handis, charcoal-grilled BBQ, fragrant biryani and pulao — alongside Arabic, Continental and Chinese favourites.

Set in a courtyard of lantern light, jali screens and fountains on MM Alam Road, Lahore, Riwayat is made for family dinners, grand buffets, private dining and rooftop evenings.
